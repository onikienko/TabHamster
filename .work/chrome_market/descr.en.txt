► Save open tabs as a group.
	✓ Tab groups are stored in Chrome sync storage. Sign in to the browser to get them on your other computers.
	✓ Hundreds of tabs per group.
	✓ Every group is also kept on your computer: if sync storage is full, the group is still saved.
	✓ Give groups names.
	✓ Edit groups, add and delete tabs, delete and move groups in the list.
	✓ Deleted groups can be restored for 30 days.
	✓ Back up your groups and settings to a file.

► Session Manager gives quick and convenient access to your latest browser sessions.
	✓ Access the current (and earlier) browser sessions.
	✓ Restore any session in one click.
	✓ !!! Sessions are not synced.


☀ Tips:
	● To quickly save all open tabs as a group, open the extension window and press Enter. You can rename the group later.
	● Ctrl + click on a group (or session) name opens its tabs in a new window.
