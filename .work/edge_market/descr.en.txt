Free and open source. No ads, no tracking.

► Save open tabs as a group, and find them again.
	✓ Press Enter to save the open tabs. Type a name first, or leave it empty and the group is named by the date.
	✓ Type to search group names, tab titles and addresses.
	✓ Groups are kept in Edge sync storage. Sign in to Edge with sync on to get them on your other computers.
	✓ Hundreds of tabs per group. Every group is also kept on your computer: if sync storage is full, the group is still saved.
	✓ Drag groups and tabs to reorder them, or drag a tab into another group.
	✓ Edit and remove tabs, add a link or the current tab, replace a group with the open tabs, rename, open in an InPrivate window, copy links.
	✓ Undo for changes and deletes. Deleted groups can be restored for 30 days.
	✓ Back up your groups and settings to a file.

► Sessions: everything that was open in your last 30 browser sessions.
	✓ Every tab and window, with the ones you closed and the pages each tab went through.
	✓ Restore a session's windows as they were, in one click.
	✓ Sessions stay on your computer: they are not synced.

► Light and dark themes.
► Everything works with the keyboard and with screen readers.

► Tips:
	• Open TabHamster and press Enter to save all open tabs as a group.
	• Click a group to see its tabs, Open (or Enter) opens them. Shift, Ctrl / ⌘ or a middle click opens them in a new window.
	• Alt (Option) + ↑ ↓ moves the selected group or tab.

► Install TabHamster from the same store on every computer you sync: copies from Edge Add-ons and from the Chrome Web Store keep separate groups. Already using TabHamster from the Chrome Web Store? Keep that copy, or move your groups with a backup file.
